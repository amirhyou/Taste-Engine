# Plan 2.2: Performance & Tuning

## Objective
Validate and optimize the engine for N=5000 items and tune the pool selection heuristics for better convergence speed.

## Context
- .gsd/phases/2/RESEARCH.md (Memory & Sort benchmarks)
- src/defaults.ts

- [ ] Engine handles N=5000 without crashing (Memory < 1GB).
