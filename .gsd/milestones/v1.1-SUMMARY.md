## Lessons Learned
- **Tight Margin Paradox**: Even conforming votes can temporarily lower stability if they are near a boundary; UI transparency (showing full ranking) is key to user trust.
- **Adaptive Selection**: Fixed boundaries fail at small N; scaling `boundaryBand` with both K and N is essential for onboarding.
- **Proactive vs Reactive Time**: Decay handles new votes, but Drift (Memory Entropy) is required for a realistic simulation of "forgetting."

Taste Engine is ready for production use as a standalone ranking library.
